# Sales CRM screens

- **Pipeline** (`/m/crm`): open opportunities as a board by stage; all leads as a paged, searchable table; a "New lead" form. A person outside every sales team sees only a note.
- **Lead** (`/m/crm/lead?id=…`): figures, notes, activities; the actions the lead's state and the viewer's role admit (edit, qualify, move stage, assign, won, lost, reopen, convert, add activity, mark done, erase contact data for administrators).
- **Settings** (`/m/crm/settings`): stages, lost reasons, teams and members; administrators also get the forms to change them.
